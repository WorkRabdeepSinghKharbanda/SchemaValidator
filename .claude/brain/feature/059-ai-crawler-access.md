# AI Crawler Access

- **Category:** Platform
- **Entry point:** `public/robots.txt`, `public/llms.txt`
- Explicitly allows major AI crawlers/agents (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, etc.) in `robots.txt`, plus an `llms.txt` plain-markdown site summary for LLM-based crawlers/agents.
